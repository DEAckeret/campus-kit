Report a website problem

Tell the web team about anything on our website that's broken, wrong or hard to use. If a problem keeps you from using a page, we'll reply within one working day.

Thanks for telling us

On a real college website, your report would now be with the web team, and you'd hear back within two working days.

College of the Sleeping Giant is a fictional school, so this demo form doesn't send anything. What you typed never left your browser.

Tell us what went wrong

Only the description is required. The more you can tell us, the faster we can fix it.

Choose "I can't use part of a page" for anything that doesn't work with your screen reader, keyboard, zoom or other setup. We fix those first.

Paste the page's address, or describe it, like "the soccer schedule."

What were you trying to do, and what went wrong?

For example, "iPhone with VoiceOver" or "Windows laptop, Chrome, zoomed to 200%."

Only if you'd like a reply. We use it to answer this report and nothing else.

This is a demo form. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

What happens next

  1. We reply

    Within one working day if the problem keeps you from using a page, and within two for everything else. If you didn't leave an email address, we can't reply, but we still read every report.

  2. We get you what you need

    If you can't use a page, we'll send you its information another way, by email or in large print, while we fix it.

  3. We fix it and tell you

    Until it's fixed, we list it under known problems on our accessibility page.

This isn't a real college

College of the Sleeping Giant is a made-up school. This website is a demo of Campus Kit, an experimental project built entirely by AI to show what a small college's website could look like.

The programs, people, events, phone numbers and email addresses are all invented. You can't apply, register or give here.
