Fall choir concert

The College Choir and the Chamber Singers perform music for the end of autumn, including a new piece by a student composer. Free and open to everyone.

When
Saturday, November 1, 7:30 to 9 p.m. Doors open at 7.
Where
St. Brigid Chapel, 410 Chapel Lane, on the north side of campus
Cost
Free
Tickets
Pick up a free ticket at the door. The chapel seats 300, first come, first served.
Add to calendar See it on the campus map

About the concert

The fall concert is the first of three Music at the Giant concerts this year. Sixty singers from every corner of the college, most of them not music majors, have rehearsed since September under director Dr. Elena Vasquez.

The concert runs about 90 minutes, with a 15-minute intermission.

What you'll hear

  • Folk songs from the northern Rockies, arranged for the College Choir
  • Renaissance music for the Chamber Singers, sung from the chapel balcony
  • The first performance of Where the Ridge Sleeps, by senior Tomas Whitehorse
  • A closing sing-along. The words are in the printed program.

Getting there

  • Parking: Lot C on Chapel Lane is free after 5 p.m. on weekends. It fills by 7:15, so the overflow is Lot A by the Admission Center, a 6-minute walk.
  • Drop-off: Cars can stop at the chapel's south door, on Chapel Lane, to let people out.
  • Walking from the center of campus: Take the path north from the library past Simms Science Center. The chapel is the stone building at the end of the path, about 5 minutes away.

Accessibility

Tell Disability Services what would make the concert work for you: email access@sleepinggiant.example or call (406) 555-0163. They can arrange most things below with a week's notice, and will always try with less.

  • Step-free entrance: Use the south door, on Chapel Lane. The main doors facing the quad have six steps and no ramp. Lot C has four accessible parking spaces beside the south door.
  • Seating: There are wheelchair spaces, each with a companion seat, at the front and the back of the chapel. Ushers hold them, and aisle seats, until 7:20.
  • Hearing: The chapel has a hearing loop. Set your hearing aid to T, or borrow a headset at the welcome table.
  • ASL interpretation: Request it by Monday, October 27.
  • Printed program: Large print copies are at the welcome table. Ask for braille by Monday, October 27. The program is also online the day before the concert.
  • Restrooms: An accessible restroom is on the lower level, reached by the elevator inside the south door.
  • Light and sound: The house lights dim but stay on. There's no strobe or haze. The chapel echoes, and the sing-along is loud. A quiet room on the lower level has the concert on speakers at low volume.
  • Service animals are welcome.

Ask for an accommodation

Contact Disability Services. Tell them which event it's for and what you need.
