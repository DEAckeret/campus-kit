    This isn't a real college

    College of the Sleeping Giant is a made-up school. This website is a demo of Campus Kit, an experimental project built entirely by AI to show what a small college's website could look like.

    The programs, people, events, phone numbers and email addresses are all invented. You can't apply, register or give here.
