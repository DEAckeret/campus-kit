Giving levels

Every gift counts toward the campaign, whatever the size. Here's what a year's giving does at each level.

  • Friend

    $50 to $249

    Covers one student's lab and studio fees for a semester. Your name appears in our annual list of donors.

  • Trailhead

    $250 to $999

    Buys a semester of textbooks for a first-generation student. You'll also get the campaign's twice-a-year update by email.

  • Summit

    $1,000 to $4,999

    Pays a student's stipend for a summer research project or unpaid internship. You're invited to the spring scholarship dinner to meet the students you support.

  • Giant's Circle

    $5,000 and up

    Funds a full one-year scholarship, named for you or for someone you'd like to honor. The student who receives it will write to you.

Why Ruth Ann Kessler gives

Ruth Ann Kessler grew up on a wheat farm 90 miles from Pine Hollow. In 1986 she arrived with one suitcase and a $600 scholarship from a donor she never met. She studied nursing, worked nights in the campus library, and graduated in 1989.

She spent 30 years as a nurse at the county hospital and has given every month since her first paycheck. Last year she set up the Kessler Scholarship for nursing students from farm and ranch families. Its first recipient, Kaylee Two Feathers, started this fall.

Other ways to give

  • By mail. Send a check made out to the College of the Sleeping Giant Foundation, 1200 College Avenue, Pine Hollow, MT 59000. Write the fund you'd like to support on the memo line.
  • By phone. Call (406) 555-0140, weekdays 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Every month. Choose "monthly" when you give online. You can change or stop it at any time.
  • Through your employer. Many employers match their staff's gifts. Ask your human resources office for a matching gift form.
  • In your will. A gift in your will or retirement account can fund scholarships for years. Our planned giving officer can talk it through with you and your lawyer.

Gifts to the College of the Sleeping Giant Foundation are tax-deductible as allowed by law. We email a receipt within one business day.
