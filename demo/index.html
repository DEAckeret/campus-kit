Sleeping Giant is a college of 1,400 students in a mountain town, with classes small enough that your professors know how you write.

Where would you like to start?

  • Apply as a first-year student

    Applications for next fall are open. No essay fee, and you hear back within three weeks.

  • Transfer your credits

    Send us an unofficial transcript and an advisor will map your credits to a Sleeping Giant degree.

  • Visit campus

    Tours run weekdays at 10 and 2, with a class visit and lunch in the dining hall.
