Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, magnification, voice control or only a keyboard. This page says how we build and check it, what doesn't work yet, and how to get help.

Our goal

We build this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. WCAG is the international standard for accessible websites, and level AA is the level that colleges and public bodies are expected to meet.

What you can expect

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, and you can always see where you are on the page. The first link on each page skips straight to the main content.
  • You can zoom to 400% without text being cut off or the page scrolling sideways. You can also change the text spacing in your browser.
  • Text stands out from its background by at least the contrast WCAG asks for (4.5 to 1), and color is never the only way something is shown.
  • Every form field has a label, and every table has a title and column headings.
  • Nothing flashes. If you've asked your device to reduce motion, the site does.
  • Pages work with JavaScript turned off.
  • Event pages say how to get in without steps, where accessible seating is, and how to ask for an interpreter or other help.

How we check

Every change to the site is checked automatically before it goes live. The checks test each page for HTML errors and common accessibility problems at desktop and phone sizes, at 200% and 400% zoom, and with wider text spacing. A change that fails any of them doesn't go live.

Automated checks find only some of the problems people run into. We also try each new page with a keyboard alone. We haven't yet gone through the site with a screen reader, and that's the next thing we'll do.

Known problems

These are the problems we know about, with what to do until they're fixed.

  • Campus map links don't open a map yet. Event pages give written directions and say which entrance has no steps.
  • Our social media graphics are pictures of text. Each one is posted with alt text that gives the same information, and the event page has it all as text.
  • The site has no video yet. When we add video, it will have captions and a transcript from the day it goes up.

Found something else? Report it and we'll add it here until it's fixed.

Getting information in another format

If you need anything on this site in another format, such as large print, plain text or braille, email the web team at web@sleepinggiant.example. Tell us the page and the format. We'll send it within three working days.

For help with a class, an event or a campus building, contact Disability Services at access@sleepinggiant.example or (406) 555-0163.

If we haven't fixed it

If you've reported a problem and you're not happy with our answer, contact the college's ADA coordinator, Maren Holt. She makes sure the college meets its duties under the Americans with Disabilities Act and Section 504, and she can tell you how to make a formal complaint, inside the college or to the federal government.
