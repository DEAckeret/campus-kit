Hollis Gallery visiting artist series

Printmaking and place

Woodcut artist June Halvorsen carves the rivers and ranch roads of the northern Rockies into blocks as large as a door. She'll talk about how a landscape changes when you have to cut every line of it by hand.

Stay after for a reception and a live printing demonstration in the studio next door.
