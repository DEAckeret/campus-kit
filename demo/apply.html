How to apply

Four steps, no application fee, and a decision about three weeks after your file is complete.

Apply in four steps

  1. Fill out the application

    Use our free online application or the Common App. Both ask for the same things, and we don't prefer one over the other. Most people finish in about 45 minutes.

  2. Send your high school transcript

    Ask your school counselor to email it to admission@sleepinggiant.example. If you've taken college classes, send those transcripts too.

  3. Add a short essay, if you'd like

    The essay is optional. If there's something your grades don't show, 250 to 500 words is plenty. Test scores are optional as well.

  4. Apply for financial aid

    File the FAFSA and list our school code, 009999. You'll be considered for every college scholarship automatically, with no separate form.

We'll email you within two days to confirm we have your application, and again if anything is missing.
