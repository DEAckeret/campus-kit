Biology

Study living systems from cells to watersheds, and start your own research before junior year.

Degree
B.S. or B.A.
Credits in the major
44
Students in research
72%

What you'll study

Biology at Sleeping Giant starts outdoors. First-year students sample the river behind campus in their second week, and that data set grows every year. From there you choose a track: ecology and field biology, cell and molecular biology, or pre-health.

Classes stay small on purpose. Labs cap at 16 students, and every biology major has a faculty mentor who helps plan courses, research and life after graduation.

What you'll be able to do

  • Design an experiment, collect data in the lab or field, and analyze it with statistics software
  • Read and critique scientific papers in your area of interest
  • Present findings to scientists and to the public
  • Meet admission requirements for medical, dental and graduate programs
